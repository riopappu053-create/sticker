Engineered Adhesion Chemistries

Substrates fail when paired with the wrong adhesive. Surface energy determines bond strength. We formulate custom tack levels suited to your application surfaces.

01

Removable Low-Tack Microspheres

Strips away from monitor bezels, laptops, and glass windows without tearing or leaving sticky adhesive behind.

02

General-Purpose Permanent Acrylic

Sets in 24 hours to form a lasting bond on smooth corrugate, paper stock, and high-energy plastics.

03

High-Surface-Tack Heavy Modified

Aggressively locks onto rough powder-coated metals, textured HDPE containers, and raw wood grain.
