Founded in 2016

Bridging Fine Typography & Industrial Adhesives.

What started as a boutique letterpress atelier in New York’s printmaking district evolved into a high-throughput industrial facility. We recognized that luxury brands were forced to choose between artisanal finishing and high-volume consistency.

By integrating multi-wavelength digital laser die-cutters with archival UV curable pigments, we created a closed-loop production space that crafts packaging labels without setup compromise.

Studio Infrastructure

Machinery That Defines Industrial Edge.

Rather than rely on generic trade-printer brokers, we house our cleanroom hardware under our direct management. We control room temperature, humidity, and curing lamps down to single-digit tolerances.

Opaque White Underbase

Over 85% opacity in a single pass ensures bright artwork vibrancy when applied to clear glass or dark substrates.

Cold Pin Curing

Low-temperature LED ultraviolet arrays secure ink dot placement without stretching heat-sensitive polymer films.

Vision Registration

In-line high-resolution CCD cameras adjust cutline paths in real-time, eliminating borders or bleed displacement.

Variable Data Engines

Sequential numbering, unique serialization, and customized QR matrices rendered on the fly during print runs.
