Standard Verification

Pre-Flight Artwork Checklist

Verified Checklist: 0 / 6 Passed

CMYK Fogra39 Profile

Save native vectors and raster assets under CMYK. Avoid RGB screen modes to eliminate color gamut clipping and unexpected shifts in tone.

Required: Color Profile

2.0mm Peripheral Bleed

Extend all edge backgrounds 2mm (0.08 in) beyond the designated trim border to prevent hairline white margins during die shifts.

Required: Cut Safety

Convert Text to Outlines

Convert typography to native vector bezier paths (`Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + O`) to prevent rasterization or missing font substitutions.

Required: Vector Assets

Native 300+ DPI Target

Raster elements like product photography must maintain at least 300 effective DPI at 100% scale to avoid soft edges and pixelation.

Required: Image Fidelity

Spot Magenta Cut Paths

Place die cutlines on a dedicated layer titled `CutContour`, formatted as a 100% Spot Magenta hairline stroke set to Overprint.

Required: CNC Die Calibration

0.5pt Minimum Line Stroke

Hairlines thinner than 0.5pt (0.175mm) or reversed light text under 6pt can choke during ink transfer and flood out on print.

Required: Stroke Weight
Mechanical Boundaries

Anatomy of a Print-Ready Die-Cut

Every label contains three strict zones. Calibrate artwork dimensions to preserve vector fidelity through rotary cutting.

Bleed Edge (+2mm)
Cut Contour (Trim Line)
Safe Zone (-2mm) Keep All Crucial Graphics & Copy Inside Here

1. Outer Bleed Boundary

+2.0 mm Beyond Trim

Background graphics, gradients, and full solid washes must extend to this outer edge. As high-speed cutters flex mechanically, bleed guards prevent raw substrate showing at margins.

2. The CutContour Vector Line

0.25 pt Spot Color Stroke

The true edge of your final physical sticker. Our Swiss CNC knives trace this exact bezier path. Ensure paths are closed geometry with no overlapping duplicate anchor nodes.

3. Internal Safe Boundary

-2.0 mm Inside Trim

Keep barcodes, logos, warning icons, and fine legal copy inside this safe area. Any element positioned outside risks clipping during high-speed production stripping.

Color Gamut Calibration

CMYK & Spot Inks Management

Computer screens emit light via RGB, whereas physical inkjet and offset heads combine cyan, magenta, yellow, and black pigment to absorb light.

Rich Black vs 100K

Standard black (K: 100%) can look washed out or gray across larger background fills. Use our custom deep black mix for bold, solid finishes.

PrintCraft Rich Black Mix:
Cyan (C):60%
Magenta (M):40%
Yellow (Y):40%
Black (K):100%
Use 100K only for body copy

Spot White Underbase

Standard process inks are naturally transparent. When printing on clear BOPP, kraft paper, or holographic stock, an opaque white ink mask keeps colors vivid.

Layer Setup Instruction:

Name layer: Spot_White

Apply 100% spot tone set to Overprint Fill to prevent underlying graphics from being masked out.

Crucial for Holographic and Clear substrates
