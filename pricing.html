Clear Policy

Pricing & Billing Clarity

Transparent parameters without hidden die, setup, or color separation surcharges.

Are there custom die-line or plate setup fees?

No. We do not charge physical die-tooling or RIP setup fees on short and mid-volume runs. Digital cutting paths are configured directly from your vector CutContour layer at zero added cost.

Can I split my volume across multiple designs?

Yes. Multiple design variations are supported within a single order. Provided every variant shares the identical cut dimension and substrate, a minor $10 artwork change fee applies per additional design.

What is included in your free shipping policy?

All continental US and Canadian orders exceeding $50 automatically qualify for complimentary ground shipping with tracking. Bulk orders over 5,000 units are upgraded to expedited priority air freight.

What if my final print doesn't match my proof?

We stand firmly behind our 100% Quality Guarantee. If your delivered product deviates from the approved digital PDF proof in registration, cut alignment, or substrate spec, we rerun the order immediately at our expense.
