Quick Clarifications

Inquiry & Sampling FAQ

Common questions regarding swatch dispatches, file audits, and physical press visits.

Are hardcopy physical press proofs available?

Yes. For high-volume packaging roll labels, we run a short 10-meter press proof on your exact selected substrate with finished laminations for a nominal setup credit refunded upon contract confirmation.

Can I arrange an in-person studio visit?

Brand owners and agency art directors are welcome to visit our Manhattan lab by appointment. Review our master archive of over 800 finished packaging labels and inspect color setups in person.

What file formats do you accept for quotes?

We accept Adobe Illustrator (.AI), vector PDFs (.PDF), EPS, and high-resolution Photoshop files (.PSD). Rough PNG or JPEG sketches can also be evaluated for preliminary ballpark quotes.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We dispatch globally via DHL Express and FedEx International Priority with full customs clearance management. Regular shipments reach European, Australian, and Asian brands within 3–5 days.
